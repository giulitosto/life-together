Notes from our conversation

Triggers & Defusing

Every person has triggers — situations that set off a disproportionate reaction, often rooted in something older than the present moment. And every trigger has a defusing mechanism: a way a partner can help, if they know what it is.

This isn't about fixing each other. It's about becoming each other's allies when the weather gets rough.
