Seeing Each Other

⏱ ~60 minutes  ·  Together

In Module 1, you each mapped your own lights and shadows — alone, honestly, privately. Now you bring those maps into the open and share them with each other.

This isn't a moment for analysis or advice. It's a moment for witnessing. To be truly seen by someone — lights and all, shadows and all — is one of the most intimate things two people can offer each other.

The invitation: Enter this with curiosity and warmth. Your partner is offering you something vulnerable. Receive it as a gift.

Steps

1

Share your lights and shadows (~30 min)

Each person shares what they wrote in Module 1 Part 3 — their lights and their shadows. Enter each other's lists below as they share. The listener listens without interrupting. 15 minutes each.
  • Listener: receive what you hear without fixing or disagreeing — just let it land
  • Sharer: be as honest as you were when you were alone

2

Celebrate each other (~20 min)

Now it's time to actively celebrate what you see. Not just acknowledge — celebrate. Look at each other's lights and name what you love about them. Then look at the shadows and name what you appreciate about those too — because the flip side of a shadow is almost always a strength.
  • What light in your partner do you most benefit from — and have you told them?
  • What shadow in your partner have you struggled with — and can you now see the strength inside it?
  • What do you want them to know about how you see them — the whole them?

3

What this opens up (~10 min)

Take a moment together to reflect on what this exercise brought up.
  • Was there anything your partner shared that you didn't fully know before?
  • Was there anything you shared that felt difficult — and how did it feel to be received?
  • How might understanding each other's shadows change how you show up for each other?
