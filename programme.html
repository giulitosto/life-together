Inside the programme

Three modules. One complete journey.

Start with yourself. Then meet each other fully. Then build the relationship you actually want.

Module 1 · Individual · ~3.5 hours
Me
Do this solo, at your own pace
  • Where I am: Wheel of Life, a full picture of your life right now
  • Who I am: your origin story and the values that drive you
  • How I am: your lights and shadows, named with honesty
  • I am enough: self-love and how you give and receive love
  • Why I am: a guided visualisation into your life purpose
Module 2 · Together · ~4 hours
You and I
Come together and really see each other
  • Land work: mapping your inner world and your partner's
  • Seeing each other: your lights and shadows, together
  • Intentions and words: designing the language of your relationship
  • Love languages together: closing the gap between you
  • Conversation cards: knowing each other, intentionally
Module 3 · Together · ~4.5 hours
Relationship
Build the relationship you want to live in
  • 1 + 1 = 3: your relationship as its own living thing
  • Our Wheel: a Wheel of Life for the relationship itself
  • Relationship Alliance: your shared compass going forward
  • Co-living: daily practices, shared space, and real adventure
  • Co-Dreaming: a guided visualisation of your shared future
