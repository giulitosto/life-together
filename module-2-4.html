Love Languages Together

⏱ ~50 minutes  ·  Together
Video coming soon

In Module 1 you each explored your own love languages — how you naturally give love, and how you most deeply receive it. Now you bring those profiles together.

Most misunderstandings in love aren't about intent — they're about translation. You might be expressing love constantly, in the language that comes naturally to you, while your partner is waiting to hear it in a completely different language. This section is about closing that gap.

Enter your Module 1 scores below — or re-rate if you'd like a fresh read. Then explore what the comparison reveals together.

Steps

1

Enter your scores (~10 min)

Each person enters their love language ratings from Module 1 Part 4 — how strongly each language resonates for giving and receiving (1 = rarely, 5 = deeply me). The comparison updates live.
2

Read the map (~15 min)

Look at the comparison together. The analysis below highlights where you're aligned and where there may be gaps worth exploring.
3

The conversation (~25 min)

Use what you've found to have an honest, curious conversation about how love moves between you.
  • When do you feel most loved by your partner — what are they doing?
  • When do you try to show love but it doesn't seem to land — what might be happening?
  • Is there a language your partner needs that you find harder to give? What would help?
  • What's one thing each of you could do differently this week?
