Love Languages Together

⏱ ~50 minutes  ·  Together
Video coming soon

In Module 1 Part 4 (I am enough) you each explored your own love languages: how you naturally give love, and how you most deeply receive it. Now you bring those profiles together.

Most misunderstandings in love and relationships aren't about intent, they're about translation and communication between humans. This section is about minimizing that gap and finding a shared love language for your relationship.

Your Module 1 scores are pulled in automatically below — adjust any slider if you'd like a fresh read. Then explore what the comparison reveals together.

Steps

1

Enter your scores (~10 min)

Your ratings from Module 1 Part 4 are pulled in automatically below, how strongly each language resonates for giving and receiving (1 = rarely, 5 = deeply me). Adjust any slider if you'd like a fresh read. The comparison updates live.
2

Read the map (~15 min)

Look at the comparison together. The analysis below highlights where you're aligned and where there may be gaps worth exploring.
3

The conversation (~25 min)

Use what you've found to have an honest, curious conversation about how love moves between you.
  • When do you feel most loved by your partner — what are they doing?
  • When do you try to show love but it doesn't seem to land — what might be happening?
  • Is there a language your partner needs that you find harder to give? What would help?
  • What's one thing each of you could do differently this week?
