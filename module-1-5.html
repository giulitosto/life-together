Why I Am

⏱ ~50 minutes  ·  Individual
Video coming soon

This is the final section of Module 1 — and perhaps the most expansive. We've explored where you are, who you are, how you are, and how you love. Now we ask the deepest question: why are you here?

This is a guided visualisation. It will take you forward in time, then further still, and bring you back with something to carry. Read it slowly. Let your mind wander where it wants to go. There are no wrong answers — only honest ones.

Before you begin: This works best when your body is still and your eyes are closed. Read each section, close your eyes, and let the images come. You can also ask someone you trust — your partner, a friend — to read it to you. That changes the experience in a beautiful way.

Choose your mode

How would you like to do this?
Read each section, then close your eyes and sit with it for a few minutes before continuing.

The Visualisation

1

Opening — settle in (~5 min)

Before anything else, arrive fully. Close this tab in your mind and open to where you are right now.
Read the opening

Find a comfortable position. Let your spine be tall, your shoulders soft. If it feels right, close your eyes.

Take a slow breath in — let your belly expand, not just your chest. Hold for a moment. And release.

— pause —

Again. Breathe in through your nose, counting to four. Hold for four. Out through your mouth for six. Let your body get heavier with each exhale.

— pause —

Notice where you're holding tension — your jaw, your shoulders, the space between your brows. And let it soften. You don't need to hold anything right now.

— pause —

You have nowhere to be for the next hour. This time belongs entirely to you.

How are you arriving? (optional)

The Journey

I
Your life at its best
~8 min
We're going forward in time. Not to a vague future — to a specific day, ten years from now, where you are living a life that is deeply yours.
Read the visualisation

Imagine waking up on an ordinary day, ten years from now. The life you've built is exactly what you hoped it would be — not perfect, but deeply yours.

— pause —

Where are you? What does the room look like? What can you hear? What's the quality of the light?

— pause —

You get up and move through your day. Notice who is around you. Notice what you're doing — the work, the rituals, the way you spend your mornings.

— pause —

This version of you feels settled. Purposeful. At home in their own skin. What does that feel like in your body right now?

— pause —

What has this person built — in their work, their relationships, themselves? What are they proud of? What do they carry lightly now that once felt heavy?

— pause —

Stay here for a moment. Let it become real.

What did you see?
II
Your contribution
~8 min
Now we look at what you've given — to the people around you, to the work you've done, to the world in some small or large way.
Read the visualisation

Still in that future day, still that ten-years-from-now version of you — think about the people whose lives are different because of yours.

— pause —

Not the grand gestures. The ordinary ones. The times you showed up. The words you said that someone carried with them. The way your presence changed a room.

— pause —

What have you built that outlasts a single day? A project, a relationship, a community, a practice, a body of work — something that bears your mark.

— pause —

If the people who love you most were to describe what you contributed to their lives, what would they say?

— pause —

And what would they not say — the quiet things you gave that you never asked to be acknowledged for?

— pause —

Notice how it feels to receive that. To let yourself be seen as someone who mattered.

What did you see?
III
Looking back
~8 min
We're going further now. To near the very end of your life — when you have the clarity that only comes with distance, and the honesty that comes with nothing left to lose.
Read the visualisation

You are old now. Your body is tired in the way that a life fully lived makes a body tired.

— pause —

You're looking back across the whole arc of your life. Not with regret — with seeing. The whole picture, from here.

— pause —

What are you glad you did? The risks you took, the loves you chose, the version of yourself you decided to become?

— pause —

What do you wish you'd done sooner? Not with guilt — just as information. A gentle correction from your oldest self to your present one.

— pause —

What actually mattered? Not what you thought would matter — what actually did, in the end?

— pause —

And what do you know now, from this vantage point, about why you were here?

What did you see?
IV
A message from your future self
~8 min
Your oldest, wisest self has something to say to you — to the you that exists right now, reading this.
Read the visualisation

That older version of you turns and looks directly at you — the you of right now, sitting here, in this moment of your life.

— pause —

They know everything you've been through. Everything you're carrying right now. Everything you're hoping for.

— pause —

And they love you. Not despite all of it — because of all of it.

— pause —

What do they want you to know? What are they trying to tell you about how to live, who to be, what to stop waiting for?

— pause —

Let them speak. Don't edit. Don't question it. Just receive what comes.

— pause —

Take your time.

What did they say?
V
Return
~5 min
Now come back. Gently, gradually, fully.
Read the closing

Begin to feel the weight of your body where you're sitting. The temperature of the air. The sounds around you.

— pause —

Take a breath in — long and slow. Hold it for a moment. And let it go completely.

— pause —

Take whatever you found in that journey and hold it gently. You don't have to understand it all right now. You don't have to do anything with it yet.

— pause —

Just bring it back with you, like something found on a long walk — something you didn't expect to find, but knew the moment you saw it that it was yours.

— pause —

When you're ready, open your eyes.

After the journey

2

Sit with it (~5 min)

Don't rush to the reflection yet. Sit quietly for a few minutes. Let what came up settle before you try to put it into words. Make yourself a drink if you'd like.
3

What emerged (~10 min)

Look back at what you noted during the visualisation. What patterns do you see? What keeps coming back?
  • What image or moment from the visualisation felt most true?
  • What surprised you — what did you not expect to find?
  • What feels urgent — something you've been putting off that your future self is asking you to begin?
What emerged
4

My Purpose Statement

Bring everything together. In one to three sentences, write what you believe to be your purpose — your reason for being here, in the fullest sense you can currently see it. It will change as you do. Write what is true right now.

You might begin with: "I am here to…" or "My purpose is to…" or simply whatever comes.
