Our Wheel

⏱ ~50 minutes  ·  Together
Video coming soon

In Module 1 you each created your own Wheel of Life. Now you look at them together — and then go one step further, rating the same areas from the perspective of your relationship as an entity.

Where are you thriving as a couple? Where is your relationship hungry for more attention? The overlapping view often reveals things that neither individual wheel could show alone.

Step 1: Each person enters their Module 1 scores (or re-rates now). Step 2: Switch to Couple view to see the overlap. Step 3: As a couple, rate how satisfied you are as a relationship in each area.

Steps

1

Review your individual wheels (~15 min)

Enter or refresh your individual scores below, then switch to Couple view to see the overlay. Take a few minutes to look at it together before saying anything.
  • Where are your wheels most aligned? Most different?
  • Which area shows the greatest gap between you — and what does that tell you?
  • What stands out that you haven't talked about before?
What you noticed in the overlap
2

The relationship wheel — how satisfied are we? (~20 min)

Now, together, rate each area from the perspective of your relationship as a whole. Not "how satisfied am I?" — but "how satisfied are we, as a couple, in this area of our life together?" Agree on a single score for each.
3

What this asks of you (~15 min)

  • Looking at your relationship wheel, which area most needs attention?
  • Which area are you most proud of as a couple?
  • If your relationship could ask one thing of each of you right now, what would it be?
  • What is one thing you could do this month to raise a score by one point?
