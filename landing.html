The programme

Three modules.
One complete journey.

Start with yourself. Then meet each other fully. Then build the relationship you actually want. Each module contains five sessions — do them at your own pace, in your own home.

Module 1 · Individual · ~3.5 hours
Me
Do this solo, at your own pace
  • Where I am — Wheel of Life
  • Who I am — Origin Story & Values
  • How I am — Lights & Shadows
  • I am enough — Self-Love & Love Languages
  • Why I am — Life Purpose
Module 2 · Together · ~4 hours
You and I
Come together and really see each other
  • Land work — My land, your land
  • Seeing each other — Lights & Shadows together
  • Intentions & Words — The language of your relationship
  • Love Languages together — Closing the gap
  • Conversation Cards — Knowing each other intentionally
Module 3 · Together · ~4.5 hours
Relationship
Build the relationship you want to live in
  • 1 + 1 = 3 — Enter the third entity
  • Our Wheel — The relationship wheel of life
  • Relationship Alliance — Your shared compass
  • Co-living — Daily practices, shared space, adventure
  • Co-Dreaming — Your shared future

Who we are

Made by a couple,
for couples

We're Giuliano and Drew — partners in life and in this work. We built Relation-ship because we wanted something that didn't exist: a couples programme for people who are already okay, but want to be extraordinary together.

Every exercise, every prompt, every session in this programme is something we have done ourselves. This isn't theory — it's practice. We've been in the waves. We know how to navigate them.

We are trained coaches based in London. Relation-ship is the programme we wish existed when we needed it most.
