Relationship Alliance

⏱ 30–60 minutes  ·  Together

The Design Relationship Alliance draws on the ORSC™ Design Team Alliance tool. It is a foundational agreement — a living document for your relationship as a third entity. It defines the culture you want together, how you navigate difficulty, and what each of you is responsible for in keeping the relationship healthy.

It isn't a rulebook. It's a compass. Return to it whenever life asks you to recalibrate.

Before you start: Read all the steps together. You can brainstorm individually first and then share, or work through it together from the start. Choose what feels right for you.

Steps

1

Read the three questions

The three columns represent the three pillars of your alliance. Read them together before you begin — make sure you both understand what each one is asking.
2

Brainstorm individually

Spend a few minutes alone adding your ideas to each column. No wrong answers — words, phrases, whatever comes.
3

Your Relationship Alliance board

Use the colour tabs to mark who added each note. Tap + Add to place your ideas.
Note colour:
What atmosphere or culture do we want to have together?
Part A: our shared culture
e.g. warmth, honesty, adventure, safety…
How do we want to be together when things are difficult?
Part A: navigating difficulty
e.g. pause before reacting, stay curious, ask not assume…
What else would help our relationship to thrive?
Part B: shared responsibility
e.g. weekly check-ins, shared rituals, saying thank you…
4

Discuss and refine together

Go through each column together. Clarify, question, rephrase until every note feels truly shared.
  • What does this mean to you specifically?
  • How will we each hold ourselves accountable to this?
  • Is there anything that needs rephrasing to feel truly ours?
5

Filter and finalise

Remove what doesn't feel essential (tap × to remove). What remains is your Relationship Alliance — a living document to return to.
